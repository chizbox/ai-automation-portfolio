# Portfolio review

Audience: small-business owners deciding in about 30 seconds whether to book a call.
Style rules: clean, warm, proof-first. Cream and terracotta (#D97757), Space Grotesk and Inter. Never suggest dark, cinematic or 3D-heavy changes. Protect: real metrics, walkthrough videos, the "Watch it run" demo, scannability.

Steps:
1. Read DONE.md in the project root (create it if missing). Never suggest anything listed there.
2. Look at the site at desktop width and 375px with a browser tool if one is available; otherwise read the code.
3. Pick a lens: the one I name, or the one least covered so far. Lenses: first impression, conversion and CTAs, copy and clarity, trust and proof, typography, color and contrast, spacing and rhythm, motion, mobile, performance, accessibility.
4. Give at most 5 suggestions ranked by impact. Each: what you saw, why it affects a visitor's decision, the exact change, and effort (quick win, medium, bigger).
5. End with the one thing a harsh critic would say that you have not said yet.
6. Do not edit files and do not invent claims about my experience, results or clients.
7. When I accept suggestions, append them to DONE.md.
